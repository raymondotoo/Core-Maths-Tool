# Chapter 31: Enlargement, Reduction and Similarity

## 31.1 Scale Factor

The **scale factor** is the ratio of corresponding lengths in similar figures.

$$\text{Scale factor} = \frac{\text{New length}}{\text{Original length}}$$

- Scale factor > 1: Enlargement
- Scale factor < 1: Reduction
- Scale factor = 1: Same size

### Example 31.1: Finding Scale Factor
#### Problem
A photograph 8 cm by 6 cm is enlarged to 20 cm by 15 cm. Find the scale factor.

#### Solution
Scale factor = 20 ÷ 8 = **2.5**
(Check: 6 × 2.5 = 15 ✓)

---

## 31.2 Center of Enlargement

An enlargement is defined by:
1. A **center of enlargement** (fixed point)
2. A **scale factor**

Each point moves along a line from the center, with distance multiplied by the scale factor.

### Example 31.2: Locating Image Points
#### Problem
Triangle ABC has vertices A(2, 1), B(4, 1), C(3, 3). Find the image under enlargement with center O(0, 0) and scale factor 2.

![Enlargement Center](www/images/chapter_31/enlargement_center.svg)

#### Solution
A' = (2 × 2, 1 × 2) = **(4, 2)**
B' = (2 × 4, 2 × 1) = **(8, 2)**
C' = (2 × 3, 2 × 3) = **(6, 6)**

---

## 31.3 Similar Figures

Two figures are **similar** if they have the same shape (equal corresponding angles and proportional sides).

### Properties of Similar Figures
1. Corresponding angles are equal
2. Corresponding sides are in the same ratio
3. The ratio of any pair of corresponding lengths equals the scale factor

### Example 31.3: Checking Similarity
#### Problem
Triangle PQR has sides 6, 8, 10 cm. Triangle XYZ has sides 9, 12, 15 cm. Are they similar?

![Similar Triangles](www/images/chapter_31/similar_triangles.svg)

#### Solution
Ratio of sides: 9/6 = 12/8 = 15/10 = 1.5

All ratios are equal, so triangles are **similar** with scale factor 1.5.

---

## 31.4 Similar Triangles

Triangles are similar if:
- **AA criterion**: Two pairs of equal angles
- **SSS criterion**: Three pairs of proportional sides
- **SAS criterion**: Two pairs of proportional sides with equal included angle

### Example 31.4: Proving Similarity
#### Problem
In triangles ABC and DEF, angle A = angle D = 70° and angle B = angle E = 55°. Prove the triangles are similar.

#### Solution
Angle C = 180° - 70° - 55° = 55°
Angle F = 180° - 70° - 55° = 55°

All corresponding angles are equal.
By AA criterion, △ABC ~ △DEF.

### Example 31.5: Finding Unknown Side
#### Problem
Triangles ABC and PQR are similar with AB = 6 cm, BC = 8 cm, PQ = 9 cm. Find QR.

#### Solution
Scale factor = PQ/AB = 9/6 = 1.5
QR = BC × 1.5 = 8 × 1.5 = **12 cm**

---

## 31.5 Area of Similar Figures

For similar figures with scale factor k:

$$\frac{\text{Area of image}}{\text{Area of original}} = k^2$$

### Example 31.6: Area Ratio
#### Problem
Two similar triangles have corresponding sides in ratio 3:5. Find the ratio of their areas.

#### Solution
Area ratio = 3² : 5² = **9 : 25**

### Example 31.7: Finding Area
#### Problem
A map uses scale 1:50000. A field appears as 2 cm² on the map. Find the actual area in km².

#### Solution
Scale factor for length = 50000
Scale factor for area = 50000² = 2,500,000,000

Actual area = 2 × 2,500,000,000 cm²
= 5,000,000,000 cm²
= 5 × 10⁹ ÷ 10¹⁰ km²
= **0.5 km²**

---

## 31.6 Volume of Similar Figures

For similar solids with scale factor k:

$$\frac{\text{Volume of image}}{\text{Volume of original}} = k^3$$

### Example 31.8: Volume Ratio
#### Problem
Two similar cones have heights in ratio 2:3. Find the ratio of their volumes.

#### Solution
Volume ratio = 2³ : 3³ = **8 : 27**

### Example 31.9: Finding Volume
#### Problem
A model car is built at scale 1:20. If the model has volume 50 cm³, find the volume of the actual car.

#### Solution
Scale factor = 20
Volume ratio = 20³ = 8000

Actual volume = 50 × 8000 = **400,000 cm³** or **0.4 m³**

---

## 31.7 Intercept Theorem

If a line is parallel to one side of a triangle, it divides the other two sides proportionally.

### Example 31.10: Intercept Theorem
#### Problem
In triangle ABC, D is on AB and E is on AC such that DE is parallel to BC. If AD = 4, DB = 6, AE = 5, find EC.

#### Solution
By intercept theorem: AD/DB = AE/EC
4/6 = 5/EC
EC = 5 × 6/4 = **7.5**

---

## 31.8 Maps and Scale Drawings

A **scale** expresses the ratio of map length to actual length.

### Example 31.11: Reading Maps
#### Problem
A map has scale 1:25000. Two towns are 8 cm apart on the map. Find the actual distance in km.

#### Solution
Actual distance = 8 × 25000 cm
= 200,000 cm
= 2000 m
= **2 km**

### Example 31.12: Drawing to Scale
#### Problem
A room is 5 m by 4 m. Draw a plan using scale 1:100.

#### Solution
Length on plan = 500 ÷ 100 = **5 cm**
Width on plan = 400 ÷ 100 = **4 cm**

## Practice Exercises

1. A shape is enlarged by scale factor 3. The original area is 12 cm². Find the new area.

2. Two similar rectangles have widths 6 cm and 15 cm. If the smaller has length 10 cm, find the length of the larger.

3. Triangle ABC has sides 5, 7, 9 cm. Triangle DEF has sides 10, 14, 18 cm.
   (a) Prove they are similar
   (b) Find the scale factor
   (c) Find the ratio of their areas

4. A map has scale 1:50000. Find:
   (a) The map length representing 3 km
   (b) The actual distance for 5.5 cm on the map

5. In triangle PQR, point S is on PQ and T is on PR with ST parallel to QR. If PS = 6, SQ = 9, PT = 8, find TR.

6. Two similar cones have volumes 27 cm³ and 125 cm³. Find the ratio of their heights.

7. A model building is made at scale 1:50. The model is 30 cm tall. Find:
   (a) The actual height
   (b) If a window on the actual building has area 2 m², find the area on the model

8. Triangle ABC is enlarged with center P and scale factor 2 to give triangle A'B'C'. If PA = 5 cm, find PA'.

9. The perimeter of a regular hexagon is 36 cm. A similar hexagon has perimeter 54 cm. Find the ratio of their areas.

10. A car has length 4.5 m. A model at scale 1:18 is made. Find the length of the model in cm.
