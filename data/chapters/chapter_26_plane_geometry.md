# Chapter 26: Plane Geometry

## 26.1 Angles

![Types of Angles](www/images/chapter_26/angle_types.svg)

### Types of Angles
| Type | Range |
|------|-------|
| Acute | 0° < θ < 90° |
| Right | θ = 90° |
| Obtuse | 90° < θ < 180° |
| Straight | θ = 180° |
| Reflex | 180° < θ < 360° |

### Angle Relationships
- **Complementary**: Sum = 90°
- **Supplementary**: Sum = 180°
- **Vertically opposite**: Equal angles

---

## 26.2 Angles on Parallel Lines

![Parallel Lines](www/images/chapter_26/parallel_lines.svg)

When a transversal crosses parallel lines:

| Angle Type | Property |
|------------|----------|
| Corresponding | Equal |
| Alternate | Equal |
| Co-interior (Allied) | Sum = 180° |

### Example 26.1: Parallel Line Angles
#### Problem
Lines AB and CD are parallel. A transversal makes angle 65° with AB. Find all angles.

#### Solution
- Corresponding angle = 65°
- Alternate angle = 65°
- Co-interior angle = 180° - 65° = 115°

---

## 26.3 Angle Properties of Triangles

- Sum of angles = 180°
- Exterior angle = Sum of two non-adjacent interior angles

### Example 26.2: Triangle Angles
#### Problem
In triangle ABC, angle A = 50° and angle B = 70°. Find angle C.

![Triangle Angles](www/images/chapter_26/triangle_angles.svg)

#### Solution
Angle C = 180° - 50° - 70° = **60°**

### Example 26.3: Exterior Angle
#### Problem
In triangle PQR, angle P = 45°, angle Q = 65°. Find the exterior angle at R.

#### Solution
Exterior angle at R = P + Q = 45° + 65° = **110°**

---

## 26.4 Congruent Triangles

Two triangles are **congruent** if they have the same shape and size.

### Conditions for Congruence:
- **SSS**: Three sides equal
- **SAS**: Two sides and included angle equal
- **ASA**: Two angles and included side equal
- **AAS**: Two angles and a corresponding side equal
- **RHS**: Right angle, hypotenuse, and one side equal

### Example 26.4: Proving Congruence
#### Problem
In triangles ABC and DEF: AB = DE, BC = EF, angle B = angle E. Prove congruence.

#### Solution
Given:
- AB = DE (side)
- Angle B = Angle E (included angle)
- BC = EF (side)

By **SAS condition**: Triangle ABC ≅ Triangle DEF

---

## 26.5 Similar Triangles

Triangles are **similar** if they have equal angles (or proportional sides).

$$\frac{AB}{DE} = \frac{BC}{EF} = \frac{AC}{DF}$$

### Example 26.5: Finding Unknown Side
#### Problem
Triangles ABC and PQR are similar. AB = 6 cm, BC = 8 cm, PQ = 9 cm. Find QR.

![Similar Triangles](www/images/chapter_26/similar_triangles.svg)

#### Solution
$$\frac{AB}{PQ} = \frac{BC}{QR}$$
$$\frac{6}{9} = \frac{8}{QR}$$
$$QR = \frac{8 \times 9}{6} = \mathbf{12 \text{ cm}}$$

---

## 26.6 Angle Properties of Quadrilaterals

Sum of interior angles = 360°

### Special Quadrilaterals:
| Shape | Angle Properties |
|-------|------------------|
| Rectangle | All angles 90° |
| Parallelogram | Opposite angles equal |
| Rhombus | Opposite angles equal |
| Trapezium | Co-interior angles sum to 180° |

### Example 26.6: Parallelogram
#### Problem
In parallelogram ABCD, angle A = 70°. Find all angles.

#### Solution
- Angle A = 70°
- Angle B = 180° - 70° = 110° (co-interior)
- Angle C = 70° (opposite to A)
- Angle D = 110° (opposite to B)

---

## 26.7 Properties of Special Triangles

### Isosceles Triangle
- Two sides equal
- Base angles equal

### Equilateral Triangle
- All sides equal
- All angles = 60°

### Example 26.7: Isosceles Triangle
#### Problem
In isosceles triangle ABC, AB = AC and angle A = 40°. Find angles B and C.

#### Solution
Let angle B = angle C = x
40 + x + x = 180
40 + 2x = 180
x = 70

**Angle B = Angle C = 70°**

---

## 26.8 Mid-Point Theorem

The line joining midpoints of two sides of a triangle is:
- Parallel to the third side
- Half the length of the third side

### Example 26.8: Mid-Point Theorem
#### Problem
In triangle ABC, M and N are midpoints of AB and AC. BC = 12 cm. Find MN.

#### Solution
MN is parallel to BC and MN = BC/2
MN = 12/2 = **6 cm**

---

## 26.9 Intercept Theorem

If parallel lines make equal intercepts on one transversal, they make equal intercepts on all transversals.

### Example 26.9: Equal Intercepts
#### Problem
Three parallel lines cut transversals at points A, B, C and P, Q, R. If AB = BC = 3 cm and PQ = 4 cm, find QR.

#### Solution
By intercept theorem: PQ = QR
**QR = 4 cm**

## 26.10 Circle Theorems Preview

Key angle properties (detailed in Chapter 30):
- Angle at center = 2 × angle at circumference
- Angles in same segment are equal
- Angle in semicircle = 90°
- Opposite angles of cyclic quadrilateral = 180°
