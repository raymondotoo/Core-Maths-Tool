# Chapter 25: Construction and Loci

## 25.1 Introduction to Geometric Construction

**Construction** uses only a compass and straightedge (ruler without measurements).

Basic constructions include:
- Copying line segments and angles
- Bisecting lines and angles
- Constructing perpendiculars
- Constructing special angles

---

## 25.2 Basic Constructions

### Construction 25.1: Copying a Line Segment
#### Problem
Copy line segment AB.

#### Method
1. Draw a ray from point P
2. Set compass to length AB
3. With center P, draw an arc to cut the ray at Q
4. PQ = AB

### Construction 25.2: Bisecting a Line Segment
#### Problem
Bisect line segment AB.

#### Method
1. Set compass to more than half of AB
2. With center A, draw arcs above and below AB
3. With center B, draw arcs to cut the first arcs at P and Q
4. Draw line PQ
5. PQ is the perpendicular bisector; M is the midpoint

### Construction 25.3: Bisecting an Angle
#### Problem
Bisect angle ABC.

#### Method
1. With center B, draw an arc cutting BA at P and BC at Q
2. With centers P and Q (same radius), draw arcs intersecting at R
3. Draw ray BR
4. BR bisects angle ABC

---

## 25.3 Constructing Perpendiculars

### Construction 25.4: Perpendicular from Point on Line
#### Problem
Draw perpendicular to line l at point P on l.

#### Method
1. With center P, draw arcs cutting l at A and B
2. With centers A and B (larger radius), draw arcs intersecting at Q
3. Draw PQ
4. PQ ⊥ l at P

### Construction 25.5: Perpendicular from Point to Line
#### Problem
Draw perpendicular from point P to line l (P not on l).

#### Method
1. With center P, draw arc cutting l at A and B
2. With centers A and B, draw arcs intersecting at Q (on opposite side to P)
3. Draw PQ
4. PQ meets l at foot of perpendicular

---

## 25.4 Constructing Special Angles

### Construction 25.6: 60° Angle
#### Method
1. Draw ray AB
2. With center A, draw arc cutting AB at P
3. With same radius, center P, draw arc cutting first arc at Q
4. Draw AQ
5. Angle BAQ = 60°

### Construction 25.7: 90° Angle
#### Method
1. Construct 60° to get point Q
2. With same radius, center Q, draw arc cutting first arc at R
3. Angle BAR = 120°
4. Bisect angle RAB to get 90°

Or use perpendicular line construction.

### Other Angles
- **30°**: Bisect 60°
- **45°**: Bisect 90°
- **120°**: Construct 60° twice
- **150°**: 180° - 30°

## 25.5 Constructing Triangles

### Construction 25.8: Triangle Given Three Sides (SSS)
#### Problem
Construct triangle ABC where AB = 6 cm, BC = 5 cm, AC = 4 cm.

#### Method
1. Draw AB = 6 cm
2. With center A, radius 4 cm, draw arc
3. With center B, radius 5 cm, draw arc cutting first arc at C
4. Join AC and BC

### Construction 25.9: Triangle Given Two Sides and Included Angle (SAS)
#### Problem
Construct triangle PQR where PQ = 5 cm, angle Q = 60°, QR = 4 cm.

#### Method
1. Draw PQ = 5 cm
2. At Q, construct 60° angle
3. Along this ray, mark R at 4 cm from Q
4. Join PR

### Construction 25.10: Triangle Given Two Angles and Included Side (ASA)
#### Problem
Construct triangle ABC where angle A = 45°, AB = 6 cm, angle B = 60°.

#### Method
1. Draw AB = 6 cm
2. At A, construct 45° angle upward
3. At B, construct 60° angle upward
4. Lines meet at C

## 25.6 Introduction to Loci

A **locus** is the set of all points satisfying a given condition.

### Standard Loci

| Condition | Locus |
|-----------|-------|
| Fixed distance from point O | Circle with center O |
| Fixed distance from line l | Pair of parallel lines |
| Equidistant from two points A, B | Perpendicular bisector of AB |
| Equidistant from two lines | Angle bisector |

---

## 25.7 Locus of Points at Fixed Distance

### Example 25.1: Circle Locus
#### Problem
Draw the locus of points 3 cm from point P.

#### Solution
Draw a **circle** with center P and radius 3 cm.

### Example 25.2: From a Line
#### Problem
Draw the locus of points 2 cm from line AB.

#### Solution
Draw **two lines parallel** to AB, one on each side, 2 cm away.

---

## 25.8 Locus Equidistant from Two Points

### Example 25.3: Perpendicular Bisector
#### Problem
Find the locus of points equidistant from A and B.

#### Solution
The locus is the **perpendicular bisector** of AB.

All points on this line are the same distance from A as from B.

---

## 25.9 Locus Equidistant from Two Lines

### Example 25.4: Angle Bisector
#### Problem
Find the locus of points equidistant from two intersecting lines l and m.

#### Solution
The locus consists of the **two angle bisectors** of the angles formed by l and m.

---

## 25.10 Combined Loci

### Example 25.5: Intersection of Loci
#### Problem
Find points that are:
- 4 cm from point O AND
- 3 cm from line AB

#### Solution
1. Draw circle with center O, radius 4 cm
2. Draw lines parallel to AB at distance 3 cm
3. The **intersection points** of the circle and parallel lines are the required points

### Example 25.6: Region Defined by Loci
#### Problem
Shade the region containing points that are:
- Less than 3 cm from P AND
- More than 2 cm from line l

#### Solution
1. Draw circle center P, radius 3 cm (inside this circle)
2. Draw parallel lines 2 cm from l (outside the region between them)
3. Shade the intersection of these regions

## 25.11 Construction of Regular Polygons

### Example 25.7: Regular Hexagon
#### Problem
Construct a regular hexagon in a circle of radius 4 cm.

#### Method
1. Draw circle with radius 4 cm
2. With same radius as compass, step around circle marking 6 points
3. Join consecutive points
4. Each side equals the radius

### Example 25.8: Square in Circle
#### Method
1. Draw circle
2. Draw diameter AB
3. Construct perpendicular diameter CD
4. Join A, C, B, D consecutively

## Practice Exercises

1. Construct a line segment of length 7 cm and bisect it.

2. Construct an angle of 75° (hint: 45° + 30°).

3. Construct triangle ABC where AB = 8 cm, BC = 6 cm, CA = 5 cm. Measure angle B.

4. Construct triangle PQR where PQ = 7 cm, angle P = 50°, angle Q = 70°.

5. Draw two points A and B, 6 cm apart. Construct the locus of points that are:
   (a) 4 cm from A
   (b) Equidistant from A and B
   (c) Find points satisfying both conditions

6. Draw a line segment AB = 8 cm. Construct the locus of points:
   (a) 3 cm from the line AB
   (b) Mark the region less than 3 cm from line AB

7. Construct a regular hexagon with side 4 cm.

8. In a rectangle ABCD, shade the locus of points that are:
   - Closer to AB than to CD AND
   - More than 2 cm from corner A
