# Chapter 29: Areas and Perimeters

## 29.1 Basic Concepts

**Perimeter**: The total length around a shape
**Area**: The amount of surface enclosed by a shape

---

## 29.2 Rectangle

$$\text{Perimeter} = 2(l + w)$$
$$\text{Area} = l \times w$$

### Example 29.1: Rectangle
#### Problem
Find the perimeter and area of a rectangle 12 cm by 8 cm.

![Rectangle Area](www/images/chapter_29/rectangle_area.svg)

#### Solution
Perimeter = 2(12 + 8) = 2 × 20 = **40 cm**
Area = 12 × 8 = **96 cm²**

---

## 29.3 Square

$$\text{Perimeter} = 4s$$
$$\text{Area} = s^2$$

### Example 29.2: Square
#### Problem
A square has area 64 cm². Find its perimeter.

#### Solution
s² = 64
s = 8 cm

Perimeter = 4 × 8 = **32 cm**

---

## 29.4 Triangle

$$\text{Perimeter} = a + b + c$$
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$$

### Example 29.3: Triangle Area
#### Problem
Find the area of a triangle with base 10 cm and height 6 cm.

![Triangle Area](www/images/chapter_29/triangle_area.svg)

#### Solution
Area = ½ × 10 × 6 = **30 cm²**

### Heron's Formula (Given Three Sides)
$$s = \frac{a + b + c}{2}$$
$$\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$$

### Example 29.4: Heron's Formula
#### Problem
Find the area of a triangle with sides 5, 6, and 7 cm.

#### Solution
s = (5 + 6 + 7)/2 = 9

Area = √(9(9-5)(9-6)(9-7))
     = √(9 × 4 × 3 × 2)
     = √216
     = **14.7 cm²**

---

## 29.5 Parallelogram

$$\text{Perimeter} = 2(a + b)$$
$$\text{Area} = \text{base} \times \text{height}$$

### Example 29.5: Parallelogram
#### Problem
A parallelogram has base 15 cm and height 8 cm. Find the area.

#### Solution
Area = 15 × 8 = **120 cm²**

---

## 29.6 Trapezium (Trapezoid)

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)h$$

where a and b are parallel sides and h is the height.

### Example 29.6: Trapezium
#### Problem
Find the area of a trapezium with parallel sides 8 cm and 12 cm, and height 6 cm.

![Trapezium Area](www/images/chapter_29/trapezium_area.svg)

#### Solution
Area = ½(8 + 12) × 6
     = ½ × 20 × 6
     = **60 cm²**

---

## 29.7 Rhombus

$$\text{Perimeter} = 4s$$
$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}d_1 \times d_2$$

where d₁ and d₂ are the diagonals.

### Example 29.7: Rhombus
#### Problem
A rhombus has diagonals 10 cm and 16 cm. Find the area.

#### Solution
Area = ½ × 10 × 16 = **80 cm²**

---

## 29.8 Circle

$$\text{Circumference} = 2\pi r = \pi d$$
$$\text{Area} = \pi r^2$$

### Example 29.8: Circle
#### Problem
Find the circumference and area of a circle with radius 7 cm. (Use π = 22/7)

![Circle Area](www/images/chapter_29/circle_area.svg)

#### Solution
Circumference = 2 × (22/7) × 7 = **44 cm**
Area = (22/7) × 7² = (22/7) × 49 = **154 cm²**

### Example 29.9: Finding Radius from Area
#### Problem
A circle has area 78.5 cm². Find the radius. (Use π = 3.14)

#### Solution
πr² = 78.5
3.14 × r² = 78.5
r² = 78.5/3.14 = 25
r = **5 cm**

---

## 29.9 Sector of a Circle

$$\text{Arc length} = \frac{\theta}{360°} \times 2\pi r$$

$$\text{Area of sector} = \frac{\theta}{360°} \times \pi r^2$$

### Example 29.10: Sector
#### Problem
Find the arc length and area of a sector with radius 10 cm and angle 72°.

#### Solution
Arc length = (72/360) × 2π × 10 = (1/5) × 20π = **4π ≈ 12.57 cm**

Area = (72/360) × π × 10² = (1/5) × 100π = **20π ≈ 62.8 cm²**

---

## 29.10 Compound Shapes

### Example 29.11: L-Shape
#### Problem
An L-shaped figure consists of two rectangles: 10 × 4 cm and 6 × 4 cm. Find the total area.

#### Solution
Area = (10 × 4) + (6 × 4)
     = 40 + 24
     = **64 cm²**

### Example 29.12: Shaded Region
#### Problem
A square of side 10 cm has a circle of radius 3 cm cut out. Find the remaining area.

#### Solution
Area of square = 10² = 100 cm²
Area of circle = π × 3² = 9π ≈ 28.3 cm²

Remaining area = 100 - 28.3 = **71.7 cm²**

---

## 29.11 Converting Units

| From | To | Multiply by |
|------|-----|-------------|
| cm² | m² | ÷ 10,000 |
| m² | cm² | × 10,000 |
| m² | hectares | ÷ 10,000 |
| hectares | m² | × 10,000 |

### Example 29.13: Unit Conversion
#### Problem
Convert 50,000 cm² to m².

#### Solution
50,000 cm² = 50,000 ÷ 10,000 = **5 m²**

---

## 29.12 Word Problems

### Example 29.14: Fencing Problem
#### Problem
A farmer wants to fence a rectangular field 80 m by 60 m. Fencing costs GH₵15 per meter. Find the total cost.

#### Solution
Perimeter = 2(80 + 60) = 280 m
Cost = 280 × 15 = **GH₵4,200**

### Example 29.15: Painting Problem
#### Problem
A room has four walls each 4 m by 3 m. Paint covers 10 m² per liter. How much paint is needed?

#### Solution
Total wall area = 4 × (4 × 3) = 48 m²
Paint needed = 48 ÷ 10 = **4.8 liters** (or 5 liters)
