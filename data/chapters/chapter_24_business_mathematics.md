# Chapter 24: Business Mathematics

## 24.1 Profit and Loss

**Basic Terms:**
- **Cost Price (CP)**: Price paid to buy an item
- **Selling Price (SP)**: Price at which item is sold
- **Profit**: SP > CP
- **Loss**: CP > SP

**Formulas:**
- Profit = SP - CP
- Loss = CP - SP
- Profit % = (Profit/CP) × 100
- Loss % = (Loss/CP) × 100

### Example 24.1: Finding Profit
#### Problem
A trader buys goods for GH₵500 and sells them for GH₵650. Find the profit and profit percentage.

#### Solution
Profit = SP - CP = 650 - 500 = **GH₵150**

Profit % = (150/500) × 100 = **30%**

### Example 24.2: Finding Selling Price
#### Problem
A shopkeeper buys a TV for GH₵2,000 and wants a 25% profit. What should be the selling price?

#### Solution
Profit = 25% of 2000 = 0.25 × 2000 = GH₵500
SP = CP + Profit = 2000 + 500 = **GH₵2,500**

Or: SP = CP × (1 + r/100) = 2000 × 1.25 = GH₵2,500

## 24.2 Discount

**Marked Price (MP)**: Original price displayed
**Discount**: Reduction from marked price
**Net Price**: Amount paid after discount

$$\text{Discount} = \text{MP} \times \frac{\text{Discount \%}}{100}$$
$$\text{Net Price} = \text{MP} - \text{Discount}$$

### Example 24.3: Calculating Discount
#### Problem
A dress marked at GH₵150 is sold at 20% discount. Find the discount and net price.

#### Solution
Discount = 20% of 150 = 0.20 × 150 = **GH₵30**
Net Price = 150 - 30 = **GH₵120**

### Example 24.4: Finding Marked Price
#### Problem
After a 15% discount, an item is sold for GH₵340. Find the marked price.

#### Solution
Let MP = x
85% of x = 340
0.85x = 340
x = 340/0.85 = **GH₵400**

## 24.3 Simple Interest

$$I = \frac{PRT}{100}$$

where:
- I = Interest
- P = Principal
- R = Rate (% per annum)
- T = Time (years)

**Amount**: A = P + I

### Example 24.5: Simple Interest
#### Problem
Find the simple interest on GH₵5,000 at 8% per annum for 3 years.

#### Solution
$$I = \frac{5000 \times 8 \times 3}{100} = \frac{120000}{100} = \mathbf{GH₵1,200}$$

### Example 24.6: Finding Principal
#### Problem
What principal will earn GH₵900 interest at 6% per annum in 3 years?

#### Solution
$$P = \frac{100 \times I}{R \times T} = \frac{100 \times 900}{6 \times 3} = \frac{90000}{18} = \mathbf{GH₵5,000}$$

---

## 24.4 Compound Interest

$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^n$$

where:
- A = Amount (Principal + Interest)
- P = Principal
- r = Rate per period
- n = Number of periods

Compound Interest = A - P

### Example 24.7: Compound Interest
#### Problem
Find the compound interest on GH₵8,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years.

#### Solution
$$A = 8000\left(1 + \frac{10}{100}\right)^2 = 8000(1.1)^2$$
$$A = 8000 \times 1.21 = GH₵9,680$$

CI = 9680 - 8000 = **GH₵1,680**

### Example 24.8: Different Compounding Periods
#### Problem
GH₵6,000 is invested at 8% per annum compounded quarterly for 1 year. Find the amount.

#### Solution
Rate per quarter = 8/4 = 2%
Number of periods = 4

$$A = 6000\left(1 + \frac{2}{100}\right)^4 = 6000(1.02)^4$$
$$A = 6000 \times 1.0824 = \mathbf{GH₵6,494.40}$$

## 24.5 Depreciation

Value decreases over time:
$$V = P\left(1 - \frac{r}{100}\right)^n$$

### Example 24.9: Depreciation
#### Problem
A car worth GH₵50,000 depreciates at 15% per year. Find its value after 3 years.

#### Solution
$$V = 50000\left(1 - \frac{15}{100}\right)^3 = 50000(0.85)^3$$
$$V = 50000 \times 0.614 = \mathbf{GH₵30,700}$$

---

## 24.6 Hire Purchase

Goods are bought with an initial deposit and installments.

**Total HP Price** = Deposit + (Number of installments × Installment amount)
**Interest** = Total HP Price - Cash Price

### Example 24.10: Hire Purchase
#### Problem
A TV costs GH₵1,200 cash. On HP: GH₵300 deposit and 12 monthly payments of GH₵90. Find:
(a) Total HP price    (b) Extra cost of HP

#### Solution
(a) Total HP = 300 + (12 × 90) = 300 + 1080 = **GH₵1,380**

(b) Extra cost = 1380 - 1200 = **GH₵180**

## 24.7 Commission

Payment based on percentage of sales:
$$\text{Commission} = \text{Sales} \times \frac{\text{Rate}}{100}$$

### Example 24.11: Commission
#### Problem
A salesman earns 5% commission. If he sells goods worth GH₵24,000, how much commission does he earn?

#### Solution
Commission = 5% of 24000 = 0.05 × 24000 = **GH₵1,200**

### Example 24.12: Salary Plus Commission
#### Problem
An agent earns GH₵800 basic salary plus 3% commission on sales above GH₵10,000. If sales are GH₵35,000, find total earnings.

#### Solution
Commission earning sales = 35000 - 10000 = GH₵25,000
Commission = 3% of 25000 = GH₵750
Total = 800 + 750 = **GH₵1,550**

## 24.8 Taxes

### Income Tax
Tax paid on earnings, often with progressive rates.

### Example 24.13: Income Tax
#### Problem
Tax rates: First GH₵5,000 at 5%, next GH₵10,000 at 10%, remainder at 15%.
Calculate tax on GH₵25,000.

#### Solution
First GH₵5,000: 5% × 5000 = GH₵250
Next GH₵10,000: 10% × 10000 = GH₵1,000
Remaining GH₵10,000: 15% × 10000 = GH₵1,500

Total tax = 250 + 1000 + 1500 = **GH₵2,750**

### Value Added Tax (VAT)
Tax added to goods and services.

### Example 24.14: VAT Calculation
#### Problem
An item costs GH₵400 before VAT. If VAT is 12.5%, find the final price.

#### Solution
VAT = 12.5% of 400 = GH₵50
Final price = 400 + 50 = **GH₵450**

## 24.9 Exchange Rates

Converting between currencies.

### Example 24.15: Currency Exchange
#### Problem
$1 = GH₵12.50. Convert:
(a) $250 to cedis    (b) GH₵5,000 to dollars

#### Solution
(a) $250 = 250 × 12.50 = **GH₵3,125**

(b) GH₵5,000 = 5000 ÷ 12.50 = **$400**

## 24.10 Wages and Salaries

### Example 24.16: Overtime Pay
#### Problem
A worker earns GH₵20 per hour for a 40-hour week. Overtime is paid at 1.5 times the normal rate. If he works 48 hours, find his total pay.

#### Solution
Normal pay = 40 × 20 = GH₵800
Overtime hours = 48 - 40 = 8
Overtime rate = 20 × 1.5 = GH₵30
Overtime pay = 8 × 30 = GH₵240

Total = 800 + 240 = **GH₵1,040**
