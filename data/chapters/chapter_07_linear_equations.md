# Chapter 7: Linear Equations in One Variable

## 7.1 Introduction to Linear Equations

A **linear equation** in one variable is an equation of the form:
$$ax + b = c$$
where x is the variable and a, b, c are constants.

### Solving Linear Equations
To solve, isolate the variable by performing the same operations on both sides.

**Key principles:**
- Add or subtract the same number from both sides
- Multiply or divide both sides by the same non-zero number

---

## 7.2 Simple Linear Equations

### Example 7.1: One-Step Equation
#### Problem
Solve x + 5 = 12

#### Solution
Subtract 5 from both sides:
x + 5 - 5 = 12 - 5
**x = 7**

### Example 7.2: Division
#### Problem
Solve 3x = 18

#### Solution
Divide both sides by 3:
$$\frac{3x}{3} = \frac{18}{3}$$
**x = 6**

---

## 7.3 Two-Step Equations

### Example 7.3: Two-Step Solution
#### Problem
Solve 2x + 7 = 15

#### Solution
Step 1: Subtract 7 from both sides
2x + 7 - 7 = 15 - 7
2x = 8

Step 2: Divide by 2
$$x = \frac{8}{2}$$
**x = 4**

### Example 7.4: Negative Coefficient
#### Problem
Solve 5 - 3x = 14

#### Solution
Step 1: Subtract 5 from both sides
-3x = 14 - 5
-3x = 9

Step 2: Divide by -3
$$x = \frac{9}{-3}$$
**x = -3**

---

## 7.4 Equations with Variables on Both Sides

Collect all variable terms on one side and constants on the other.

### Example 7.5: Variables on Both Sides
#### Problem
Solve 4x - 3 = 2x + 9

#### Solution
Step 1: Subtract 2x from both sides
4x - 2x - 3 = 9
2x - 3 = 9

Step 2: Add 3 to both sides
2x = 12

Step 3: Divide by 2
**x = 6**

### Example 7.6: More Complex
#### Problem
Solve 7x + 5 = 3x - 11

#### Solution
Step 1: Subtract 3x from both sides
4x + 5 = -11

Step 2: Subtract 5 from both sides
4x = -16

Step 3: Divide by 4
**x = -4**

## 7.5 Equations with Brackets

Expand brackets first, then solve.

### Example 7.7: Single Bracket
#### Problem
Solve 3(x - 4) = 15

#### Solution
Step 1: Expand
3x - 12 = 15

Step 2: Add 12 to both sides
3x = 27

Step 3: Divide by 3
**x = 9**

### Example 7.8: Multiple Brackets
#### Problem
Solve 2(3x + 1) - 3(x - 2) = 17

#### Solution
Step 1: Expand all brackets
6x + 2 - 3x + 6 = 17

Step 2: Simplify left side
3x + 8 = 17

Step 3: Subtract 8
3x = 9

Step 4: Divide by 3
**x = 3**

---

## 7.6 Equations with Fractions

Multiply through by the LCM of denominators to clear fractions.

### Example 7.9: Simple Fraction
#### Problem
Solve $$\frac{x}{3} + 5 = 9$$

#### Solution
Step 1: Subtract 5 from both sides
$$\frac{x}{3} = 4$$

Step 2: Multiply both sides by 3
**x = 12**

### Example 7.10: Multiple Fractions
#### Problem
Solve $$\frac{x}{2} + \frac{x}{3} = 10$$

#### Solution
LCM of 2 and 3 is 6

Step 1: Multiply every term by 6
$$6 \times \frac{x}{2} + 6 \times \frac{x}{3} = 6 \times 10$$
$$3x + 2x = 60$$

Step 2: Simplify
5x = 60

Step 3: Divide by 5
**x = 12**

### Example 7.11: Fraction with Expression
#### Problem
Solve $$\frac{2x-1}{4} = \frac{x+3}{2}$$

#### Solution
Step 1: Multiply both sides by 4
$$2x - 1 = 2(x + 3)$$
$$2x - 1 = 2x + 6$$

Step 2: Subtract 2x from both sides
$$-1 = 6$$

This is false! **No solution** (the equation is inconsistent)

## 7.7 Word Problems

Translate words into algebraic equations.

### Key Words:
| Words | Operation |
|-------|-----------|
| sum, total, increased by | + |
| difference, less than, decreased by | - |
| product, times, of | × |
| quotient, divided by | ÷ |
| is, equals, gives | = |

### Example 7.12: Age Problem
#### Problem
John is 5 years older than Mary. Their combined age is 37 years. Find their ages.

#### Solution
Let Mary's age = x
Then John's age = x + 5

Equation:
x + (x + 5) = 37
2x + 5 = 37
2x = 32
x = 16

**Mary is 16 years old**
**John is 21 years old**

### Example 7.13: Number Problem
#### Problem
The sum of three consecutive integers is 84. Find the integers.

#### Solution
Let the integers be x, x + 1, x + 2

Equation:
x + (x + 1) + (x + 2) = 84
3x + 3 = 84
3x = 81
x = 27

**The integers are 27, 28, and 29**

### Example 7.14: Money Problem
#### Problem
A pen costs GH₵3 more than a pencil. If 4 pens and 3 pencils cost GH₵45, find the cost of each.

#### Solution
Let pencil cost = x cedis
Then pen cost = (x + 3) cedis

Equation:
4(x + 3) + 3x = 45
4x + 12 + 3x = 45
7x + 12 = 45
7x = 33
x = 4.71 (approximately)

However, using exact fractions:
7x = 33
$$x = \frac{33}{7} = 4\frac{5}{7}$$

**Pencil costs GH₵4.71** (approximately)
**Pen costs GH₵7.71** (approximately)

## 7.8 Equations with Unknown in Denominator

### Example 7.15: Variable in Denominator
#### Problem
Solve $$\frac{12}{x} = 4$$

#### Solution
Multiply both sides by x:
12 = 4x

Divide by 4:
**x = 3**

### Example 7.16: Complex Denominator
#### Problem
Solve $$\frac{6}{x-2} = 3$$

#### Solution
Multiply both sides by (x - 2):
6 = 3(x - 2)
6 = 3x - 6
12 = 3x
**x = 4**

## 7.9 Literal Equations (Changing Subject)

Rearrange equations to express one variable in terms of others.

### Example 7.17: Simple Subject Change
#### Problem
Make x the subject of y = 3x + 5

#### Solution
Step 1: Subtract 5
y - 5 = 3x

Step 2: Divide by 3
$$\mathbf{x = \frac{y-5}{3}}$$

### Example 7.18: Variable Appears Twice
#### Problem
Make x the subject of ax + b = cx + d

#### Solution
Step 1: Collect x terms on one side
ax - cx = d - b

Step 2: Factor out x
x(a - c) = d - b

Step 3: Divide by (a - c)
$$\mathbf{x = \frac{d-b}{a-c}}$$
